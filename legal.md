# Why not to talk to the police, ever
- There is no way it can help.
- You may admit your own guilt with no benefit in return (even if not guilty).
- You can easily get carried away and tell some little lie or make some little mistake that can be used to prove you are untrustworthy by being wrong or outright lying.
- If the police don't recall the testimony with 100% accuracy, it can harm you.
- If the police don't recall the questions with 100% accuracy, it can harm you, EVEN IF RECORDED.
- Some other evidence or testimony can claim that part of your testimony or answer is false.

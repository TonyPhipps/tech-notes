# Timing
- Glass an hour before sunrise
- Hunt as early and as late as possible (Hunting pressure pushes them further into nocturnal)
- Go enjoy camp during the day, or scout out your next glassing

# Location
- 1-2 miles off dirt roads (but likely near highways due to no pressure)
- Under thick, dark cover (they react to hunting pressure by going nocturnal)

# Positioning
- Mind Thermals
  - In mornings, warm air rises, so smell drifts up hills. Deer are more likely to be higher. Don't be below them.
  - In evenings, warm air is pushed downward, so does smell. Deer are more likely to be lower. Don't be above them.
- Consider prevailing winds with thermals

# Glassing
- Glass the timber like a sniper (30min+ easily) for something as small as an ear flicker.

# Elk

## Tracks
- Bull - 4 knuckles at widest point
- Cow - 3 inches at widest point

## Scat
- About 1 inch long
- Bull is often as wide as it is long
- Cow is thinner, more oval

## Calling
- Bull Bugling
  - to call Bulls
- Cow Mew
  - To call cows
  - Shorter, dropping off at the end (lower pressure at end)
